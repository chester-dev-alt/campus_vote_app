Campus Voting Application – Project Summary

Project Manager: Blessing Mhlanga

The Campus Voting Application project was developed to provide a secure, efficient, and transparent digital voting platform for university students. As the Project Manager, I was responsible for coordinating a team of 10 members, ensuring smooth communication, task allocation, and timely completion of project milestones.

From the beginning, the project followed an organized development lifecycle, including planning, design, implementation, testing, and deployment. Regular team meetings were conducted to track progress, resolve challenges, and ensure alignment with project objectives.
